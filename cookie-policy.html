Magzest Consultancy Services ("Magzest," "we," "us") operates www.magzest.tech. This Cookie Policy explains which cookies and similar technologies the website uses, why we use them, and how you can control them. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

In short: our own cookies are only the ones the website needs to work. We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not use cookies to track you across other websites. The live chat on every page is provided by Zoho SalesIQ, which sets its own cookies when the page loads (see section 3). Our visitor statistics are collected without cookies.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. They let the site remember information between page loads, such as a choice you have made or the fact that you are signed in. "Similar technologies" include browser storage such as sessionStorage and localStorage, which work in much the same way. This policy covers both.

First-party cookies are set by www.magzest.tech itself. Third-party cookies are set by another company's service embedded in our pages.

1. Strictly necessary cookies

These cookies are needed for the website to work and to keep it secure. Because the site cannot function properly without them, they do not require your consent under the GDPR and the EU ePrivacy rules, and they cannot be switched off from the cookie banner. You can still block or delete them in your browser (see below), but some features may then stop working.

2. Performance and analytics

We want to know which pages are useful and how visitors find us, but we measure this without cookies and without any third-party analytics service:

  • Page views are counted on our own server at the moment a page is delivered. Nothing is stored in your browser.
  • To estimate unique visitors, we combine your IP address and browser user-agent with a random value that changes every day and is never saved. The result is a short one-way hash, so we cannot recover your IP address from it. Because the random value is discarded daily, visits on different days cannot be linked together.
  • We record the referring website or campaign tag (for example utm_source) as a total per day, not per person.
  • Automated traffic such as search-engine crawlers is excluded, and the data is reported only as daily totals.

We do not currently set any performance or analytics cookies. If we ever introduce them, they will be off by default, set only after you give consent through the cookie banner, and listed on this page before they are used.

3. Third-party content

Some pages embed content from other providers. Those providers may set their own cookies or read device information when the content loads or when you interact with it. We do not control these cookies, and they are governed by the provider's own privacy and cookie policies.

  • Zoho SalesIQ live chat (all pages): lets you chat with our team. The chat widget loads with every page, and Zoho sets its own cookies and similar storage to keep your conversation connected, to recognise you as a returning visitor, and to show our team which pages you are viewing while you chat. See Zoho's cookie policy.
  • Google Maps (Contact page): shows our office location. The map loads only when you click "Show map"; from then on, Google may set cookies. See Google's cookie policy.
  • YouTube (Vlogs page): videos are embedded in YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com), which does not store tracking cookies until you play a video. Once you press play, YouTube may store information on your device.
  • Google Fonts and code libraries (unpkg CDN): these deliver typefaces and scripts. They do not set cookies, but like any web server they receive your IP address in order to deliver the files.

4. Your consent and how to change it

When you first visit the site, a banner explains how we use cookies. Because our own cookies are all strictly necessary, there is nothing optional to accept or decline, so the banner simply asks you to acknowledge it (OK, got it). That acknowledgement is stored in the magzest-cookie-consent cookie so the banner doesn't reappear on every page.

If we ever introduce optional cookies (such as analytics), the banner will offer a genuine choice to Accept or Decline them, they will stay off until you accept, and you will be able to withdraw your consent at any time as easily as you gave it. 5. Controlling cookies in your browser

Most browsers let you see, block and delete cookies, either for all websites or for specific ones. Instructions for common browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari (Mac) and Safari (iPhone and iPad)

Embedded services never load on their own: if you don't click "Show map" on the Contact page or play an embedded video, Google and YouTube cannot set cookies through our site. The Zoho live chat is different: it loads with every page, so to stop its cookies, block or delete cookies for zoho.in and zohopublic.in in your browser. If you block all cookies, the site will still work, but the cookie banner will reappear on each visit and the admin area will not let staff sign in.
