Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Magzest Consultancy Services ("Magzest," "we," "us") operates www.magzest.tech. This page explains what information we collect through this website, why, and what choices you have.

Information we collect

We collect information you choose to give us directly:

  • Contact form: name, email, phone (optional) and your message.
  • Careers application form: name, email, the role you're applying for, your resume/CV file, and any message you add.
  • Consultation booking: name, email, and the date/time/service you select.
  • Newsletter signup: your email address.

We do not run analytics on this site, and we do not use third-party advertising trackers (see our Cookie Policy). We don't sell or rent your information to anyone.

Cookies

This site uses one first-party, strictly functional cookie:

  • magzest-cookie-consent — remembers your Accept/Decline choice on the cookie banner, so we don't ask again.

This cookie is not used for advertising or cross-site tracking. You can clear cookies at any time through your browser settings. Our Cookie Policy lists every cookie and similar technology the site uses.

Third-party services

This site loads a small number of external resources to function: Google Fonts (typefaces), the Zoho SalesIQ live chat on every page (which sets its own cookies; see our Cookie Policy), a Google Maps embed on our Contact page, and CDN-hosted libraries for styling, icons and animation (Tailwind CSS, Lucide, AOS). These providers may process your IP address as part of delivering that content, under their own privacy policies.

Form submissions are delivered to our team by email through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co). Contact enquiries are also added to our customer records in Zoho Bigin. Both services handle the information under their own privacy policies.

How we use your information

We use the information you submit only to respond to your inquiry, process your job application, confirm a booking, or send the newsletter you signed up for. We keep it only as long as reasonably needed for those purposes.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time. To do so, email us at contact@magzest.tech.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page from time to time as the site or our practices change. The "last updated" date at the top will reflect the most recent revision.
